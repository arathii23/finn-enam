**Financial interpretation must be a gate before prose generation.** These are proposed product rules, informed by the inspected documents; they have not been implemented.

| Situation | Required behaviour |
|---|---|
| Consolidated versus standalone | Store entity scope with each number. Prefer the group basis when matching group guidance, but never silently substitute one basis for the other. Also distinguish total PAT from profit attributable to owners. HCLTech's FY25 table shows INR17,399 crore total profit versus INR17,390 crore attributable to owners: two valid bases, not conflicting values. [Audited table, PDF p. 2](https://www.hcltech.com/sites/default/files/documents/investor-reports/Audited-Financial-Results-for-the-quarter-and-year-ended-March-31-2025.pdf). |
| Units and currency | Preserve original value/unit/currency and show any normalisation. One crore is 10 million; one lakh is 100,000. Do not compare nominal INR, nominal USD and constant-currency growth as equivalent. Use percentage points for differences between rates. |
| Different financial periods | Record explicit start/end dates and fiscal-year identity. A year labelled FY25 need not mean calendar 2025. Do not compare three-month growth with an annual forecast or a balance-sheet point in time with a period flow. |
| Quarter versus year-to-date | Label both. Derive a quarter from cumulative figures only when the entity, definition and accounting basis match; label it derived and link both inputs. Derive Q4 from full year minus nine months only under those checks. Never average four growth percentages to claim annual growth. |
| Restatements/amendments | Preserve original and replacement filings, publication timestamps and the supersession relationship. Current research can use the amended comparable series; historical views may use only amendments published by their cutoff. Flag unresolved conflicts rather than mixing vintages. |
| Missing/non-comparable metrics | Use not disclosed, not found, extraction failed and incomparable as distinct states. None means zero or missed. Keep EBIT, EBITDA, operating profit, company revenue and services revenue separate. Omit optional metric tiles rather than filling gaps from model knowledge. |
| Conditional/revised guidance | Retain speaker, original words, target period, conditions and all located revisions. Evaluate the latest applicable range available before the outcome, while showing the revision trail. A superseded original range must not be silently treated as the final expectation. |
| Source typos/extraction errors | Check relevant table headers, footnotes and another company disclosure. HCLTech's January transcript includes an implausible cash-flow unit/decimal string elsewhere on the guidance page. Do not silently repair it or let a cited typo become a confident fact. |
| Ambiguous outcomes | Separate observed disclosure, calculated comparison and interpretation. Abstain when the matching tuple fails. No company rankings or credibility scores without a defined and separately validated methodology. |

**The revised scope contains exactly two workflows.**

1. **Review what changed:** an in-app portfolio queue, default 24-hour window with date selection, explicit priority reasons, routine filings collapsed, all source documents accessible, coverage states, and reviewed/keep-watching actions. Add simple configurable daily price/volume thresholds once a sufficient baseline is available. They are EOD checks, not intraday alerts. Prioritisation is a rule to inspect, not a claim about likely stock-price impact.
2. **Investigate a company or event:** the same research view opened from an event or a general ticker entry. It provides business context from an annual report, relevant management commentary from transcripts, current primary disclosures where relevant, and one unresolved question. Add a numerical expectation/outcome comparison only when all matching fields pass; otherwise explain the gap.

Cut automatic universal guidance verdicts, management scores, broad financial agents, peer comparisons, personalised thesis monitoring, biographies, native/OS notifications, voice, portfolio accounting and exhaustive multi-year ingestion. Do not cut general ticker entry, primary-source discipline, working underlying feeds or deployment verification.

**Three uncertainties should determine the next implementation decision.**

| Unresolved uncertainty | Fastest practical check | Consequence of failure |
|---|---|---|
| Hosted access and document discovery beyond two IT companies | From the intended host, repeat feed + attachment + annual-report/transcript downloads for the two verified companies and cold-start two other-sector tickers. Verify document identity and one useful passage per document. Timebox the first attempt. | Repair the source path before UI polish. A cached showcase cannot establish generalisation. |
| Reusable financial matching without company-specific interpretation | Give the extractor the same fields for both verified examples and one non-IT example: entity, metric, unit/currency, period, statement type, conditions, revisions and page evidence. Manually check the result. | Remove automated expectation/outcome judgments; retain source-grounded briefing and research. |
| Investor time saved and attention quality | Run the five-event task test against exchange/IR pages. Measure elapsed time, correct identification of the key change, source-opening effort and missed material items. | Shorten/reorder the workflow; if it still adds little value, replace the review concept before extending it. |

**Recommendation for the final specification:** Proceed with FINN as a mobile portfolio review queue connected to general, primary-source company research. Use 15 holdings, three prepared research examples once verified, a dated historical replay and a separate cold ticker path. Treat expectation checks as an optional capability gated by metric, entity, period and qualification matching. Show evidence and missing coverage directly. Ship the working briefing and research path first; retain automated comparisons only if reusable extraction and investor time savings pass the early checks.
